Board border: O = Outer, I = Inner.

O is the perimeter of the 4 × 7 board: 0–7, 13, 14, 20–27 (18 numbers). I is the center: 8–12 and 15–19 (10 numbers).

Outer absence counts consecutive I results; inner absence counts consecutive O results. Outer streak counts consecutive O results; inner streak counts consecutive I results. Different numbers with the same letter extend the run. The opposite letter resets it. Alerts fire once when an enabled rule reaches its threshold in verified, uninterrupted history. Stars and New/Repeated labels are separate from this condition.
